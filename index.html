02 — Why me

I don’t design at your audience.
I design for the tribe.

I

I speak the language.

Hard work, purpose, brotherhood. I know what those words mean to a nineteen-year-old who just decided to change his life. A great brand says them without ever having to write them.

II

Aesthetics is a discipline.

The rules you apply to the body apply to a logo. Cut the excess. Master the fundamentals. Let proportion do the talking. Nothing decorative survives my process.

III

Built for every surface.

Merch, app icon, thumbnail, gym wall, book spine. Each mark is tested at 16 pixels and at 16 metres, in gold foil and in one-colour embroidery, before it reaches you.

IV

No ego. Just reps.

Clear process, fast iterations, honest feedback both ways. You get options, the reasoning behind them and production-ready files. No excuses, no disappearing acts.

03 — The Process

Trained like a programme.

Four phases. Every one ends with something you can see, judge and sign off.

  1. Phase 01

    Purpose

    We define who the brand serves, what it stands against and what it must make people feel.

  2. Phase 02

    Forge

    Dozens of sketches, a handful of directions. Only the strongest three reach you.

  3. Phase 03

    Refine

    One direction, pushed until every angle, weight and gap is deliberate.

  4. Phase 04

    Deliver

    Full file system, colour and type rules, and mockups on the surfaces your audience actually sees.
